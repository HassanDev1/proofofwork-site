Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL BUSINESS NAME] (“we,” “us,” or “our”), the provider of the ProofOfWork mobile application (the “App”), collects, uses, stores, and shares information. ProofOfWork helps tradespeople and field professionals document work with timestamped photos, voice notes, weather records, materials, and — where the user chooses — customer authorizations and signatures.

The short version. Most of your data — your jobs, photos, voice notes, weather records, materials, and payment notes — is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded to us. The main exception is the optional remote‑signing feature: when you choose to send a signing link to a customer, the details needed for that signature are stored on our cloud infrastructure so the customer can review and sign. Details below.

1. Information We Collect

a. Information stored only on your device

The following are created and stored locally on your phone in the App’s on‑device database. We do not receive, transmit, or have access to this information unless you use a feature that explicitly sends it (see Section 2):

  • Job and project details you enter (titles, site/project names, locations, scope, prices, notes).
  • Photos you capture within the App, including their timestamps and any GPS location attached to them.
  • Voice notes you record.
  • Weather records fetched for a job (see weather, below).
  • Materials, payment notes, and settings, including any business logo or brand colors you add.
  • For standard (non–Field Log) jobs, customer contact details you choose to enter (name, phone, email, address).

If you delete the App or lose, reset, or replace your device, on‑device data cannot be recovered by us. We recommend exporting or sharing your PDF reports to keep permanent copies.

b. Information sent to our cloud infrastructure (remote signing)

When you choose to request a signature or approval from a customer using a remote signing link, the App sends the following to our backend (hosted on Supabase) so the customer can open a secure web page and sign:

  • Customer name, and the email address and/or phone number you entered for delivery.
  • Job details relevant to the authorization (such as scope of work, agreed price, payment terms, start date, and location).
  • The customer’s signature image and the date/time they sign, once submitted.

This information is provided by you, the App’s user, and we act as the data controller for it. Supabase acts as our data processor, storing this information on our behalf on their cloud infrastructure. If you never use the remote‑signing feature, none of this information leaves your device through this feature.

c. Device permissions

PermissionWhy the App uses it

d. Weather data

When you record weather for a job, the App sends your device’s GPS coordinates to Open‑Meteo, a third‑party weather service, to retrieve current conditions for that location. The returned weather data is then stored on your device with the job.

e. Subscriptions and purchases

Paid subscriptions are processed by the app store you purchased through (Google Play or the Apple App Store) and managed through a subscription‑management provider (RevenueCat). We do not collect or store your credit card or payment details; those are handled by the app store. We may receive your subscription status (for example, whether you are on a free or Pro plan) to unlock features.

f. Push notification token

To deliver signing notifications, the App may register a push notification token with the Expo push notification service and associate it with your signing requests so we can notify you when a customer signs.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide the App’s core features (documenting jobs, generating reports).
  • To deliver remote signing links to your customers and return their signatures to you.
  • To send you notifications you have asked for (such as signing alerts).
  • To determine your subscription status and provide the features included in your plan.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How Information Is Shared

We share information only as needed to operate the App:

  • Service providers (processors): Supabase (storage of remote‑signing data), Open‑Meteo (weather lookups), the Expo push service (notifications), our hosting provider Vercel (the signing web page), and RevenueCat and the app stores (subscription processing).
  • Your customers: when you send a signing link, the customer you send it to can view the job details you included in order to review and sign.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

4. Data Retention

On‑device data remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.

Remote‑signing data stored on our cloud infrastructure is retained until you delete the associated job or request deletion (see Section 6). Unused signing links expire 48 hours after they are created. We may periodically remove old or completed signing records to limit the data we hold; less stored data means less risk to you.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, including secured connections and access controls on our cloud infrastructure. Signature files are stored in our provider’s storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting access to your own device.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Because most data lives on your device, you control it directly: edit or delete jobs, photos, and notes in the App, or uninstall the App to remove all local data.

For information stored on our cloud infrastructure through the remote‑signing feature, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that information by contacting us at the email in Section 10. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and as required by applicable law.
