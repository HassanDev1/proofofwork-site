ProofOfWork

Document your work. Protect your business.

Timestamped photos, verified conditions, and signed agreements — the record you need when a job is questioned.

What it does

📸 Before / During / After Photos

Capture timestamped, GPS-tagged photos at every stage of a job, organized automatically.

✍️ Customer Signatures

Get agreements signed on-site or remotely, so scope and price are documented before work begins.

🌦️ Verified Conditions

Weather and location are recorded automatically, adding credibility to your documentation.

📄 Professional Reports

Generate polished PDF reports and share a complete record of any job in seconds.

💵 Payment Tracking

Track agreed prices, deposits, and installments — see exactly what's paid and what's owed.

📊 Accounting Export

Export your income records to a spreadsheet, ready for your books or your accountant.

Who it's for

ProofOfWork is built for independent contractors, tradespeople, and field engineers who need a reliable record of the work they do.

When a customer disputes what was agreed, what was done, or what they owe, your documentation is your protection. ProofOfWork makes creating that documentation fast enough to actually do on every job.
